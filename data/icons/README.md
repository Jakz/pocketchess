# App icons for the reader's launcher

`pocketchess.bmp` (normal) and `pocketchess_f.bmp` (focused): 8-bit grayscale BMP, 106x128, the
DejaVu Sans knight (U+265E) over a board strip. On firmware 6.8+ they go to `applications/icons/` and
are referenced from `system/config/desktop/view.json` (entry `U_pocketchess`). Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later
(drawn from DejaVu Sans, Bitstream Vera licence).
